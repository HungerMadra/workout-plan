Robi built this for you. There is nothing to set up. Every day, three small things:
1
Food. Eat today's plan, then tap Mark meals as done. Close enough counts.
2
Walk. Type your steps from your phone's Health app. Aim for 10,000.
3
Water. Tap a glass each time you finish one.
+
Workout days (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat): follow the list and tick the boxes. Tap ▶ to see how a move is done.
That's the whole app. Everything saves on its own.
Tip: add this to your home screen so it opens like an app. iPhone: Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: menu, then Add to Home screen.
