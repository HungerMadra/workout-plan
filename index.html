🎯 WEIGHT LOSS REALITY
Losing 5–10 lbs/month is achievable but requires both exercise + caloric deficit. 1 lb of fat = ~3,500 calories burned or cut. This plan handles the burn side — aim to also cut 500–700 cal/day from food for the best results.
🥗 NUTRITION BASICS
✅ Eat more protein — chicken, eggs, fish, beans (~120g/day)
✅ Cut liquid calories — sodas, juice, alcohol are the biggest saboteurs
✅ Eat whole foods — veggies, rice, oats, healthy fats
✅ Don't skip meals — 3 meals + 1–2 light snacks
✅ Calorie target: ~1,500–1,700 cal/day for your stats
💧 HYDRATION
Drink 2.5–3 litres of water daily. Start every morning with a big glass before coffee. Water suppresses appetite, boosts metabolism, and speeds recovery.
😴 SLEEP & RECOVERY
Aim for 7–9 hours of sleep. Poor sleep raises cortisol (the stress hormone) which stores belly fat. Treat sleep as seriously as workouts.
📈 MONTHLY PROGRESSION
Month 1 (Foundation): Learn movements. Focus on form. Build the habit.
Month 2 (Build): Numbers increase ~30%. You'll feel noticeably stronger.
Month 3 (Intensity): Full output — this is where the transformation shows.
⚖️ DUMBBELL GUIDE
Upper body: Start with 5–8 lbs per dumbbell
Lower body: Start with 10–15 lbs (lunges, squats, deadlifts)
Rule: Last few reps should feel hard — if not, go heavier
Month 3: Increase weight by 2–5 lbs from Month 1
